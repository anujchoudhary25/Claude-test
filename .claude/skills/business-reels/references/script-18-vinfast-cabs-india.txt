Bhai, Vietnam ka sabse ameer aadmi India mein 10,000 cabs lekar aa gaya hai… aur yeh dekhkar Ola, Uber ke saath-saath Mahindra aur Tata ki bhi tension badh gayi hai.
Toh exactly ho kya raha hai? Dhyaan se suno.
Pehle inka model samajhte hain.
Vietnam ki EV company VinFast apna cab platform India mein lekar aayi hai. But Ola aur Uber ki tarah inke drivers apni khud ki gaadi nahi laate.
Company khud unhe apni electric SUVs deti hai, aur drivers ko milti hai around ₹25,000 se ₹40,000 tak ki fixed monthly salary.
Ab dekho bhai, inka asli game kya hai.
Normal car companies kya karti hain? Crores of rupees TV ads, billboards aur showrooms mein spend karti hain… sirf isliye ki tum unki car ko 15 minute ke liye test drive karo.
Maruti, Hyundai, Tata — sab broadly isi model par kaam karte hain.
Ab VinFast ne bola — hum yeh sab nahi karenge.
Hum apni car ko hi ek chalta-phirta showroom bana denge.
Drivers ko proper training di ja rahi hai ki ride ke beech mein customer ko car ke features explain karein.
Aur is ride mein customer khud AC feel kar sakta hai, car ka comfort experience kar sakta hai aur electric range ko practically samajh sakta hai.
Aur bhai, sabse dangerous baat?
Customer inki marketing dekhne ke liye khud inhe paise de raha hai.
Matlab… negative customer acquisition cost!
Ola aur Uber ka main business hai rides sell karna.
Lekin VinFast ka core business hai cars sell karna.
Isliye inhone cab service ko sirf ek revenue model nahi, balki ek self-funding marketing channel bana diya hai.
Toh rides par achha-khasa discount dena bhi inke liye koi badi baat nahi hogi.
Aur ek aur interesting cheez samjho.
Yeh log yeh model sirf India ke liye nahi kar rahe.
VinGroup ne is model ko Vietnam, Lagos, Philippines aur Indonesia jaise markets mein already test kiya hai.
Aur ab India unka fifth market hoga.
Matlab yeh koi random experiment nahi hai.
Yeh ek tested playbook hai.
