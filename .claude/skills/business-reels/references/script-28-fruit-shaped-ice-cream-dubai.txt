Yeh simple sa idea Dubai mein viral ho raha hai!
Ek normal shop jahan aapko ek hi flavour ki ek badi ice cream milti hai…
Uske instead, yeh shop sell karti hai ek box full of mini ice creams…
Jo exactly fruits ki tarah dikhti hain!
Mango…
Strawberry…
Kiwi…
Aur Lemon!
Toh jaise hi aap box open karte ho…
Aisa lagta hai jaise aapke saamne colorful fruits ki ek basket rakhi ho.
Lekin jaise hi aap bite lete ho…
Pata chalta hai ki andar hai creamy, fruity ice cream!
Aur sabse interesting baat?
Isse banane ka process surprisingly simple hai.
Yeh log real fruits ka pulp aur juice lete hain…
Usmein sugar aur cream mix karte hain…
Phir usse freeze karte hain…
Aur finally fruit-shaped silicon molds ki help se unhe exact fruits ka shape de dete hain.
Result?
Product dikhta hai super premium…
Aesthetic hai…
Aur extremely Instagram-friendly bhi!
Isi wajah se log ise online share karna pasand karte hain…
Aur brand ko free mein marketing mil jaati hai.
Lekin yahan hai asli business insight.
Aaj ke time mein log sirf taste ke liye food nahi kharidte.
Log experience ke liye kharidte hain…
Presentation ke liye kharidte hain…
Aur Instagram par share karne ke liye bhi kharidte hain.
Aur agar koi India mein iska ek properly branded version launch kar de…
Toh yeh easily ek viral dessert brand ban sakta hai.
Aise hi unique business ideas ke liye…
Mujhe daily follow karna mat bhoolna!
