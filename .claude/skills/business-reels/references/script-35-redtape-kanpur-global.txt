Kanpur ki galiyon se nikla yeh brand…
Aaj Adidas aur Nike jaise global brands ko bhi takkar de raha hai.
Normally Indian companies kya karti hain?
Pehle India mein product test karti hain…
Market samajhti hain…
Aur phir international expansion ke baare mein sochti hain.
Lekin 1996 mein Red Tape ne iska bilkul opposite kiya.
Inhone Kanpur ke decades-old leather manufacturing experience ka advantage liya…
Aur shoes ko directly UK aur US markets mein launch kar diya.
Ab socho…
Ek Indian brand jo apne home market mein properly establish bhi nahi hua tha…
Woh directly global market mein enter kar raha tha.
Aur isi move ki wajah se…
Red Tape ko starting se hi ek strong global brand positioning mil gayi.
Phir 1998 mein…
Inhone finally India mein entry li.
Aur yahan inhone ek massive market gap identify kiya.
Indian youth ko international-looking sneakers chahiye the…
Lekin global brands ki pricing kaafi high thi.
Red Tape ne yahin ek smart strategy use ki…
Premium positioning + affordable pricing.
Matlab product ka look aur feel premium…
Lekin price relatively accessible.
Aur result?
Ek completely Indian company…
Aaj reportedly thousands of crores ki valuation ke saath…
37+ countries mein operate kar rahi hai.
Is story ka biggest lesson simple hai…
Kabhi-kabhi apne home market mein compete karne se pehle…
Global market ko target karna bhi ek powerful positioning strategy ho sakti hai.
