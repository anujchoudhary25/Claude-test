Aur simply freeze kar deta hai.
Aur kuch hi hours baad…
Iske paas ready hote hain dozens of colorful aur healthy fruit popsicles!
Sabse interesting baat yeh hai ki isne yeh business apne ghar se start kiya tha.

Fresh dikhte hain…
