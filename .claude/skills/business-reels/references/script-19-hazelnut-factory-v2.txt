Kyuki inka game sirf mithai bechne ka nahi tha.
Inka game tha — har gali mein milne wali normal mithai ko premium kaise banaya jaaye?
Sabse pehle inhone product aur menu engineering bahut smartly ki.
Inhone normal halwai-style ke laddoo nahi beche.
Inhone laddoo ke fusion versions create kiye.
Usmein chocolate, hazelnut jaise international ingredients add kiye.
Aur usko sirf Chocolate Laddoo ya Blueberry Laddoo bolkar nahi chhoda.
Usko naye, premium-sounding English names diye.
Unke names ko trademark bhi karaya aur ek completely new category create kar di.
Aur menu mein in products ke combos bhi traditional lassi ya doodh ke saath nahi…
Balki speciality coffee ke saath banaye.
Ab iska direct impact pada inki pricing aur average order value par — jo food business ka ek bahut important metric hai.
Yeh log around ₹2,000 per kg ki mithai sell karte hain.
Lekin inki ek aur smart strategy dekho.
