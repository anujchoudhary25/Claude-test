2015 mein literally 36,000 tonnes Maggi ko jala diya gaya tha.
Us time sabko laga tha…
India ki favourite two-minute noodles company shayad ab permanently khatam ho gayi.
Jab Maggi par ban laga…
Toh market mein unka almost 80% market share overnight disappear ho gaya.
Lekin bhai…
The rise and fall of Maggi ki story ka sabse bada twist abhi baaki tha.
Maggi ne competitors se ladne ke bajaye…
Seedha customer ke emotions ko target kiya.
Unhone launch kiya…
"We Miss You Too" campaign.
Aur us waqt Maggi ne sirf noodles sell nahi kiye…
Unhone nostalgia aur trust sell kiya.
Unhone apne safety testing process ko bhi public ke saamne transparent rakha…
Taaki customers ko clearly pata ho ki product ke saath actually kya ho raha hai.
Aur result?
Sirf 5 months ke andar Maggi ne itna strong comeback kiya…
Ki yeh comeback corporate history ki most talked-about marketing victories mein count hone laga.
Ab iska business lesson bahut simple hai.
Crisis ke time panic karne ke bajaye…
Trust build karo.
Kyuki jab customers ka trust aapke saath hota hai…
Toh ek temporary crisis bhi brand ko permanently destroy nahi kar sakta.
Is evergreen branding lesson ko yaad rakhne ke liye…
Reel ko save kar lo.
Aur aisi hi business stories ke liye…
Mujhe follow karna mat bhoolna.
