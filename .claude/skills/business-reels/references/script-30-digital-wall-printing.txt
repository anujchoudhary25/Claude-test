India mein yeh business abhi bahut kam log kar rahe hain…
Lekin aane wale time mein yeh business boom kar sakta hai!
Yeh hai Digital Wall Printing Machine ka business.
Basically, yeh machine kisi bhi wall ya surface par directly high-quality 3D designs print kar sakti hai.
Matlab aapko wallpaper lagane ya manually painting karne ki zaroorat nahi…
Machine directly wall par design print kar degi.
Aur yahan hai iska asli business opportunity.
Aaj market mein wall printing ka rate around ₹200 se ₹300 per square feet tak ja sakta hai…
Lekin is machine se printing ki cost sirf around ₹10 per square feet tak aa sakti hai.
Matlab agar aapko ek project mein achha volume mil gaya…
Toh margins kaafi high ho sakte hain.
Sirf 2-3 decent-sized walls print karke hi aap around ₹1 lakh tak revenue generate kar sakte ho.
Aur is business ko start karne ke liye machine ki cost roughly ₹5 lakh ke aas-paas ho sakti hai.
Matlab ek baar machine mein investment karke…
Aap homes, cafes, restaurants, offices, hotels aur retail stores ke liye customized wall printing services de sakte ho.
Aur sabse interesting part?
Aaj bhi India mein is service ko offer karne wale businesses relatively kam hain.
Toh agar aap early entry lete ho…
Proper marketing aur unique designs ke saath…
Toh yeh ek interesting business opportunity ban sakti hai.
Aise hi unique business ideas ke liye follow karna mat bhoolna!
