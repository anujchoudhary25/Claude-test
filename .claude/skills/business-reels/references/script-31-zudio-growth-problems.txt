Zudio ka sabse bada dushman koi aur brand nahi hai…
Khud Zudio ki growth hai.
Tata ne aggressive expansion karke almost 1,000 Zudio stores open kar diye.
Sunne mein toh yeh ek massive success story lagti hai…
Lekin exactly yahin se start hoti hain inki do sabse badi problems.
Pehli problem — Brand Fatigue.
Aaj college mein har teesra banda wahi ₹500 wali same Zudio T-shirt pehenkar ghoom raha hai.
Aur Gen Z ko kya chahiye?
Exclusivity.
Jab ek hi design har jagah dikhne lage…
Toh brand instantly uncool feel hone lagta hai.
Ab second problem — Quality.
Audience ko ab samajh aa gaya hai ki 3-4 washes ke baad inka fabric kaafi jaldi kharab ho sakta hai.
Aur bas isi weakness ka fayda uthaya…
Reliance ke Usta aur Shoppers Stop ke Intune ne.
Yeh brands bhi affordable pricing par fresh designs offer kar rahe hain.
Matlab customer ke paas ab alternatives hain…
Aur woh bhi similar price range mein.
Result?
Zudio ka per-square-foot revenue aur same-store growth pressure mein aane laga hai.
Isi wajah se metro cities mein saturation ke baad…
Zudio ab aggressively Tier-3 aur Tier-4 cities mein expand kar raha hai.
Jahan Tata ka trust aaj bhi ek strong advantage hai.
Lekin founders ke liye yahan ek bahut clear lesson hai.
Agar extreme volume expansion ke chakkar mein…
Aap quality aur customer retention ko ignore kar dete ho…
Toh market share lose hone mein bilkul time nahi lagta.
Scale important hai…
Lekin scale without retention ek dangerous game hai.
Business ki aisi hi deeper insights ke liye…
Mujhe follow kar lo.
