Tamil Nadu ka yeh aadmi kachre se furniture banakar crores kama raha hai.
B.L. Begani ne saalon tak plywood ka business kiya.
Lekin unhe ek din realize hua…
Furniture banane ke liye jo trees cut kiye ja rahe hain, uska climate aur environment par bahut bura impact ho raha hai.
Is realization ke baad…
Unhone apni plywood company tak sell kar di.
Aur nikal pade ek mission par…
Wood ka sustainable alternative dhoondhne ke liye.
Iske liye unhone Japan, Korea aur Europe tak travel kiya.
Aur finally unhe mila ek aisa raw material…
Jise hum almost waste samajhte hain.
Rice Husk.
Yaani chawal ka woh chhilka…
Jise India mein kai farmers simply jala dete hain.
Aur yeh crop burning pollution ka ek major contributor hai.
B.L. Begani ne is rice husk ko collect karke dry kiya…
Uska powder banaya…
Aur phir usmein minerals aur polymer resin mix kiya.
Aur is process se banaya…
NFC Board.
Dekhne mein yeh bilkul wood jaisa lagta hai…
Lekin performance mein traditional wood se kaafi better hai.
Kyuki yeh…
Waterproof hai.
Termite-proof hai.
Fire-retardant hai.
Aur recyclable bhi hai.
Matlab ek aisa product…
Jo furniture industry ko sustainable banane mein help kar raha hai…
Aur saath hi agricultural waste ko useful product mein convert kar raha hai.
Aaj inki company Indo Wood isi NFC board ko sell karke around ₹50 crore ki company ban chuki hai.
Aur sabse interesting baat…
Yeh business sirf paisa nahi kama raha…
Balki pollution aur waste ko reduce karne mein bhi ek important role play kar raha hai.
